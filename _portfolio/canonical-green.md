Canonical Green is a climate-tech startup that is built on our weather-routing research. Its product, [Green Navigation](/portfolio/green-navigation/), works like a Google Maps for the sea: it uses weather and ocean data to choose routes that use less fuel. I'm the COO.

Some milestones:

- **Opentop acceleration program** (2023). Our first pilot, with Boluda Towage, simulated and optimized real tugboat routes using validated consumption models. In the simulations we cut GHG emissions by about 10% on those routes.
- **Missions València 2030** (2023). I took the project to the call and pitched it to the jury. It was ranked first and received €93,362.84, awarded to Canonical Green.
- **European Parliament** (June 2023). I pitched Green Navigation there through The Arch programme.
- **Prototypes for Humanity** (Dubai, 2024). Green Navigation was selected among 100 innovations from more than 2,000 submissions.
- **The Conversation Weekly** (2025). I talked about Green Navigation and low-emission shipping on the podcast.

More at [canonicalgreen.com](https://canonicalgreen.com).
