Outside my research and teaching, I have been making short films for several years. These projects have given me a different way to explore storytelling, visual communication, and collaborative work.

Short Films

▶

Discordias Primaverales

My first short film, made as a parody of classic black-and-white cinema. I helped produce the film and played the father. It won Best Film in our school competition.

Roles: Actor and producer

▶

El Ruido del Silencio

Filmed on New Year's Day, it uses an unusually empty Madrid—and no special effects—to tell an introspective story about being alone. It won second place in the Comillas University film competition.

Roles: Story, lead actor, and editor

▶

Tick Tock Bang

A silly, playful little film presented at Notodofilmfest.

Content note: References to suicide.

Roles: Story, lead actor, and editor

▶

Y de repente yo

A short musical film presented in the special edition of Notodofilmfest held during the COVID-19 lockdown.

Roles: Story and editor
