Hello dear readers,

On 16 December 2024, a student I co-directed defended their master's thesis at the University of Cádiz, in the Research Master in Systems and Computing Engineering. It was graded Sobresaliente.

The title, translated: *Magnetic field interpolation techniques with machine learning in space-based gravitational-wave observatories*. The problem is to estimate the magnetic field at places where there is no sensor, using measurements from other places.

Congratulations to the student, and thanks to everyone at Cádiz who made it possible.

All the best,

Daniel Precioso, PhD
