Hello dear readers,

Teaching is the part of my job where I like to go beyond the classroom. A few examples.

**Counselor days.** I present the Bachelor of Applied Mathematics to school counselors. After one of those sessions a counselor came to me and asked, "Do you teach there?" and then said, "University needs more professors with that kind of passion." I'll keep that one.

**Datathons.** I mentor students in the IE sustainability datathons. It's a good place to see students apply what they learn in class to a real problem in a weekend.

I also run data workshops, and I like how different the audiences are: counselors deciding where to send their students, first-years who have never coded, and teams who have a weekend to build something.

All the best,

Daniel Precioso, PhD
